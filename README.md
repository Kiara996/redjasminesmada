# 🌺 Red Jasmine Smada — SMAN 2 Madiun Informatics Final Project 🌺

Welcome to the **Red Jasmine Smada** project!  
This is a collaborative website created as the final exam for the Informatics subject (Kelas Informatika) by 12th grade students of SMAN 2 Madiun, academic year 2024-2025.

---

## 📝 Project Overview

Each student in the class built and published their own personal website, showcasing their 🧑‍🎓 biography, 🏆 achievements, and 🎨 creative works.  
The main domain serves as a homepage and gallery for the "Red Jasmine" generation, the class of 2022 who will graduate in 2025.

> **Original Purpose:**  
> Originally, this website was made to promote the 71st anniversary of SMAN 2 Madiun. Over time, its purpose evolved into becoming the official site for MPK OSIS SMAN 2 Madiun and a long-term platform for student portfolios, projects, and class memories.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Personal Web Pages:**  
  Every student contributed a page about themselves, including hobbies, achievements, and their own creative content.  
  👤 🏅 🎮 🎶 📝

- **Showcase of Projects:**  
  Projects include:
  - 🎥 Short movies made with PCC Smada & English Club Smada
  - 🎵 Music and writing shared on external platforms like YouTube and Kwikku
  - 📰 Blogspot-powered news portal for Jasmine Smada events
  - 🖥️ Portfolio websites and web apps

- **Homepage & Gallery:**  
  The main domain displays a gallery of the Red Jasmine class.  
  🏫 🎓
